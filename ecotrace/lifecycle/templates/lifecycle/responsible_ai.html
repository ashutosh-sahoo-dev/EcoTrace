AI governance

Responsible AI & Governance

EcoTrace uses AI as a decision-support capability, not as an unquestionable authority. The system is designed around explainability, data minimization, fairness and human accountability.

Core governance rule

AI recommends. Humans remain accountable.

An AI recommendation should support campus operations teams rather than automatically trigger physical retirement, disposal or recycling. Consequential lifecycle actions should remain subject to operational review.

⚖️

Fairness

Recommendations use operational asset attributes rather than protected personal characteristics.

  • ✓ No protected personal characteristics are requested.
  • ✓ Decisions are tied to device condition and operational status.
  • ✓ Human review can challenge AI output.
🔍

Transparency

Each asset stores a decision, diagnosis, maintenance priority, environmental rationale, confidence and AI source/model.

  • ✓ Rationale is stored with the lifecycle passport.
  • ✓ Confidence is visible to operators.
  • ✓ Live Granite and fallback decisions are distinguished.
🔐

Privacy

The workflow is intentionally designed around asset data rather than personal information.

  • ✓ Minimize data sent to AI services.
  • ✓ Never place API credentials in source code.
  • ✓ Use environment variables for secrets.
🧭

Ethics

EcoTrace follows a life-extension-first principle while recognizing that responsible recycling is necessary for genuine end-of-life assets.

  • ✓ Repair before replacement where reasonable.
  • ✓ Recycle end-of-life electronics responsibly.
  • ✓ Keep consequential decisions under human oversight.

Project description

EcoTrace and UN SDG 12

EcoTrace is an AI-powered electronic asset lifecycle management prototype for educational campuses. It creates a digital passport for each device, applies AI-assisted maintenance and end-of-life reasoning, and records sustainability impact metrics.

Its sustainability objective is to support responsible consumption by extending useful device life, reducing premature replacement, improving traceability, and directing end-of-life electronics toward responsible recycling pathways.
